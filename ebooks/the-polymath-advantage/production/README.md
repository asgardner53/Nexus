## Production doctrine

No new concepts.
No new chapters.
No broad redevelopment.
No opportunistic expansion.

A production change must do at least one of two things:

1. remove a publication blocker; or
2. materially improve reader comprehension, consistency, accessibility or production quality.

If it does neither, defer it until after publication.

- Epistemic Mobility
- Polymathic Judgement
