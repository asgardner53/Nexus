# The Polymath Advantage — P3 Five-Figure Family Review

Status: COMPLETE
Scope: first-generation candidates for Figures 4.1, 5.1, 8.1, 9.1 and 10.1
Purpose: assess visual-family coherence and check each figure against the approved P3 wireframe before artwork freeze.

## Executive verdict

The five generated candidates establish a promising common visual language, but they are **not yet ready to freeze as publication masters**.

The strongest candidates are Figures 5.1 and 9.1. Figures 4.1, 8.1 and 10.1 drift materially from the approved wireframes by introducing additional concepts, process steps or framing that the manuscript does not require.

This is exactly what the family review is designed to catch.

The correct next move is not to keep regenerating everything. It is to revise only the three figures that have drifted, then conduct one final family consistency check.

## Family-level strengths

Across the five candidates:

- clean white/light backgrounds work well;
- navy headings create a strong visual anchor;
- teal/green/purple accents are coherent;
- figure numbering is clear;
- the overall tone is sophisticated and professional;
- the graphics are recognisably part of one family;
- visual hierarchy is generally strong;
- key propositions are easy to locate;
- the diagrams are substantially more useful than decorative images would be.

## Family-level risks

### 1. Too much embedded text

Several candidates contain enough prose to become mini-slide decks rather than ebook figures.

Final figures should use the visual to carry the relationship and leave detailed explanation to the surrounding chapter.

### 2. New-theory creep

Some generated panels introduce concepts such as purpose, values, integrity, wisdom, empathy, shared responsibility or generic collaboration principles that are sensible in themselves but are not the specific learning job of the approved figure.

The figure must illustrate the manuscript, not extend it.

### 3. Kindle readability

Several current labels will become too small at approximately 600-pixel-wide display.

Final artwork should reduce text before production freeze.

### 4. Authenticity footer clutter

The current generated designs often place authenticity notes inside the artwork.

Production recommendation:

- keep the visual clean;
- place figure title/caption and evidence/synthesis note in the book layout immediately below the image rather than embedding a dense authenticity footer inside the artwork.

This also improves accessibility and reusability.

## Figure-by-figure verdicts

## Figure 4.1 — Epistemic Mobility

### Candidate strength

The current artwork strongly communicates movement and anchoring.

### Drift from approved wireframe

The approved anchor was:

**depth • evidence • standards**

The generated candidate replaces that with:

- purpose;
- values;
- ethics;
- context;
- judgement.

Those are reasonable leadership concepts, but they materially change the chapter's intellectual point.

Chapter 4 is about changing problem representation without losing disciplinary/evidentiary grounding. It is not a values-and-purpose framework.

The generated cycle also adds:

- See Differently;
- Think Deeply;
- Act Wisely;
- Learn Continuously;

which makes the figure look like a broader capability-development model rather than a visual of Epistemic Mobility.

### Required revision

Return to the simpler approved architecture:

- central **PROBLEM**;
- 4–5 surrounding lenses;
- deliberate movement among lenses;
- fixed anchor: **DEPTH • EVIDENCE • STANDARDS**.

Keep the caption:

> **Change the lens without losing the anchor.**

Verdict: **REVISE BEFORE FREEZE.**

## Figure 8.1 — Building a Polymathic Practice

### Candidate strength

The recursive visual character is clear and visually attractive.

### Drift from approved wireframe

The approved chapter argument deliberately rejected a rigid staged system and specified a simple five-node loop:

**Problem -> Explore -> Compare -> Test -> Revise -> Problem**

The generated candidate instead creates a seven-stage model:

- Attend;
- Gather;
- Analyse;
- Synthesise;
- Judge;
- Act;
- Reflect.

It also introduces:

- wisdom in service of purpose;
- integrity at the centre;
- curiosity with direction;
- discernment and responsibility;
- multiple practice principles.

This risks doing precisely what the chapter warns against: turning polymathic practice into a branded routine.

### Required revision

Return to the approved five-stage recursive loop:

**PROBLEM -> EXPLORE -> COMPARE -> TEST -> REVISE -> PROBLEM**

Use only four small boundary callouts:

- depth;
- evidence;
- competence;
- specialist input.

Caption:

> **Breadth becomes useful when the problem pushes back.**

Secondary line in layout, not artwork if space is tight:

> **Practise the missing capability, not the identity.**

Verdict: **REVISE BEFORE FREEZE.**

### Required revision

Simplify final artwork to:

- AI-EXPANDED REACH;
- dashed boundary: ACCESS CAN EXTEND BEYOND COMPETENCE;
- VERIFY gate;
- ACCOUNTABLE JUDGEMENT / ACTION.

Keep the current funnel idea only if it remains legible and does not imply the chapter formally defines five validated filters.

Verdict: **APPROVE CONCEPT; SIMPLIFY FOR FINAL ARTWORK.**

## Figure 10.1 — Collective Polymathy and Selective Permeability

### Candidate strength

The figure communicates diversity, filtering and collaboration attractively.

### Drift from approved wireframe

The approved figure's core point was not generic diverse teamwork. It was:

- specialist domains remain bounded;
- only relevant knowledge pathways open;
- the shared consequential problem sits at the centre;
- expertise gaps can route outward;
- decision authority remains explicit.

The generated candidate instead emphasises:

- diverse minds;
- collaboration principles;
- constructive friction;
- shared responsibility;
- filtering noise.

Those ideas belong to the broader chapter, but they weaken the precise visual thesis:

> **Preserve the boundary. Open the pathway.**

### Required revision

Return to the approved architecture:

- five separately bounded specialist domains;
- central **SHARED CONSEQUENTIAL PROBLEM**;
- selected dashed pathways only;
- **DECISION AUTHORITY** node;
- optional external expertise pathway when a gap is identified;
- simple key: boundary preserved / pathway opened / gap escalated.

Do not depict every specialist connected to every other specialist.

Verdict: **REVISE BEFORE FREEZE.**
