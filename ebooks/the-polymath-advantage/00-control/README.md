# The Polymath Advantage

## Working title
The Polymath Advantage: Why Breadth, Depth and Connection Matter in the Age of AI

Author: Alec Gardner
Publisher: Nexus BMG Pty Ltd
Status: Live ebook development

## Governing thesis
The polymathic advantage is not knowing everything. It is developing the capacity to move responsibly across knowledge boundaries while remaining anchored by depth and disciplined by judgement.

As knowledge expands, specialisation becomes increasingly necessary. Greater specialisation also fragments knowledge. AI expands intellectual access but does not automatically create understanding, competence or judgement. The modern polymathic professional therefore needs enough depth, intellectual range, integration and judgement to recognise relevant expertise, interrogate unfamiliar knowledge, integrate perspectives and know where competence ends.
