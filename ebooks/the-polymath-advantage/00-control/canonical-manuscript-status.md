## Governing thesis

The polymathic advantage is not knowing everything. It is developing the capacity to move responsibly across knowledge boundaries while remaining anchored by depth and disciplined by judgement.

The finished manuscript develops this proposition from the individual specialist, through cross-domain movement and judgement, into AI-expanded access, distributed team expertise, organisational knowledge movement and finally the person who can move responsibly through what no one person can know alone.

## Concept hierarchy

### Umbrella term

**polymathic capability** — the book-level capacity to move responsibly across knowledge boundaries while remaining anchored by depth and disciplined by judgement.

### Signature constructs

1. **Epistemic Mobility** — deliberate movement among different ways of framing, questioning and interpreting a problem while remaining aware of the assumptions and limits of each perspective.
2. **Polymathic Judgement** — author synthesis. The capacity to integrate knowledge across domains while calibrating confidence, preserving competence boundaries and bringing deeper expertise into the judgement when one's own understanding reaches its limits.
3. **Collective Polymathy** — author synthesis. The capacity of a group to make differentiated specialist depth usable together around a shared problem.

### Named chapter-level synthesis

**Borrowed Breadth** — Chapter 9 synthesis describing access to concepts, language and provisional synthesis from domains in which the user does not personally possess equivalent depth.

### Light organising principle

**selective permeability** — Chapter 11 design principle describing the need to preserve useful knowledge boundaries while opening pathways for relevant knowledge to move.

Do not promote every useful phrase to signature-construct status.
