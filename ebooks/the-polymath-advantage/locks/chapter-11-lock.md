## Locked central proposition

Organisational polymathic capability is the capacity of an organisation to preserve differentiated depth while moving relevant knowledge across boundaries so that complex problems can be reframed, integrated and acted on responsibly.

This is author synthesis.

## Selective Permeability status

Selective Permeability survives as author synthesis with explicit intellectual ancestry.

It is not presented as a replacement for knowledge management, absorptive capacity, knowledge transfer, boundary spanning or knowledge integration.

Its narrower explanatory role is to address the book's central organisational tension: the same boundary that creates specialist depth, accountability and reliable execution may also be the boundary that a consequential problem needs relevant knowledge to cross.

## Protected lines

- The same boundary that protects depth can also block transfer.
- An organisation can drown in access while starving for understanding.
- The objective is not maximum flow. It is relevant flow.
- Relevant knowledge needs a path not only into conversation but into authority.
- The organisation needs connective capacity, not a single connective genius.
- The polymathic organisation is not one in which knowledge moves freely. It is one in which knowledge can move intelligently.
