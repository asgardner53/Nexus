The core manuscript spine is strong.

1. dismantle the universal-genius myth;

3. define movement among frames as Epistemic Mobility;

7. translate the evidence into problem-centred practice;

9. scale the capability beyond the individual through Collective Polymathy.

Chapters 11 and 12 remain coherent with the governing thesis, but the final architecture changed materially during development.

This requires a canonical architecture update before final whole-book review.

## Finding 1 — the reconstructed architecture is now stale

The locked reconstructed architecture originally specified:

- Chapter 11: Creation at the Boundaries
- Chapter 12: The Polymath Advantage

The current manuscript instead contains:

- Chapter 11: Organisations Where Knowledge Can Move
- Chapter 12: The Person Who Can Move

This was not random drift. The later chapters evolved toward a stronger scale progression:

individual capability -> AI-expanded access -> team-level integration -> organisational knowledge movement -> final human synthesis.

That sequence is coherent and arguably stronger than the earlier creation chapter because it completes the movement from individual cognition to distributed knowledge systems.

However, the control architecture still describes Creation at the Boundaries as the intended payoff.

Required action:

Update the canonical manuscript architecture to describe the book actually written rather than auditing the finished manuscript against a superseded plan.

## Finding 2 — the disappearance of the standalone creation chapter needs an explicit decision

The original architecture expected a dedicated payoff chapter on creation as disciplined integration.

The current manuscript distributes that function instead:

- Chapter 5 treats structural transfer and analogical generation;
- Chapter 8 treats output/creation as a test of integration;
- Chapter 9 treats AI-generated connections and the need for discrimination;
- Chapter 10 treats integrated team decisions and synthesis;
- Chapter 11 treats organisational knowledge movement into consequential decisions;
- Chapter 12 resolves the value proposition around responsible movement.

Stage 1 judgement:

A standalone creation chapter is not obviously missing from the finished book.

The book's governing promise is breadth, depth, connection and responsible movement, not creativity itself. Adding a creation chapter now could feel like a late detour and disturb the final progression.

But the whole-manuscript pass should explicitly test whether the reader receives enough evidence of what polymathic capability produces in practice: better reframing, better questions, improved integration, better decisions, designs, hypotheses or outputs.

Do not add Chapter 13 or reopen Chapter 11 merely because the old architecture contained Creation at the Boundaries.

## Finding 3 — Chapter 2 to Chapter 10 progression is exceptionally clean

The chapter sequence changes the governing question rather than simply adding topics.

Chapter 2 asks what polymathy is not.
Chapter 3 asks what useful breadth requires.
Chapter 4 asks how a person changes representation.
Chapter 5 asks how structure transfers.
Chapter 6 asks where transfer stops and how confidence is calibrated.
Chapter 7 asks what is developable.
Chapter 8 asks what disciplined practice looks like.
Chapter 9 asks what AI changes when breadth becomes easier to access.
Chapter 10 asks how capability scales when depth is distributed across people.

No structural reorder is indicated at Stage 1.

## Finding 4 — Chapter 11 is a legitimate organisational-scale extension of Chapter 10

Chapter 10 asks how differentiated specialist depth becomes usable inside a team.

Chapter 11 asks whether the organisation allows relevant knowledge to cross the boundaries created by specialisation.

The distinction is real:

- Chapter 10 owns integration among people around a shared problem.
- Chapter 11 owns organisational pathways, absorptive capacity, networks, incentives, decision authority and selective permeability.

The current Chapter 11 therefore earns its place and should not be treated as a replacement chapter that weakened the original architecture.

## Finding 5 — Chapter 12 resolves the individual/system tension effectively

Chapter 12 returns to the person after Chapters 10 and 11 have made expertise explicitly distributed.

This is structurally valuable.

The final answer is no longer a heroic polymath who personally integrates everything. The professional remains anchored in depth, capable of movement, able to recognise limits and able to participate in systems where important knowledge lives in other people and institutions.

The final line is consistent with the book-level thesis:

The polymathic advantage is not knowing everything. It is being able to move responsibly through what no one person can know alone.

Stage 1 verdict: final chapter placement and role PASS.
