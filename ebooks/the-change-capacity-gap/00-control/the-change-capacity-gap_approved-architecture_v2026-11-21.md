## Controlled concept decisions

### Change absorption capacity

Use this preferred definition:

> **Change absorption capacity is the available human and organisational capacity to take in, make sense of, integrate and sustain a given level of change while continuing to perform core work without unacceptable degradation in judgement, execution, trust or learning.**

This is an Alec Gardner integrative working definition informed by, but not equivalent to, established constructs such as organisational change capacity, organisational change capability or absorptive capacity.

### Performance feedback trap

Retain as an original interpretive model. It may be supported by adjacent research on performance feedback and self-reinforcing restructuring, but must not be presented as an established universal causal mechanism.

### Stabilisation and recovery

The book should emphasise **stabilisation and consolidation**. Recovery is one human component of the broader organisational requirement to embed routines, rebuild depleted resources, resolve ambiguity and prevent incomplete change from becoming permanent background load.

### AI

AI is not a detachable technology chapter. It is a contemporary stress test of the central argument: an organisation's capacity to initiate change can accelerate faster than its capacity to absorb that change.

## Architecture boundaries

The book must not become:

- a generic change-management manual;
- a resilience or burnout book;
- a project-management guide;
- a catalogue of transformation frameworks;
- a claim that all organisations are change saturated; or
- a claim that change capacity can be reduced to one universal numeric score.

Each chapter must make a distinct contribution to the whole-book argument.

## Approval scope

Architecture approval authorises:

- chapter evidence briefs;
- research and evidence mapping;
- case-study development;
- chapter drafting;
- chapter-level developmental review; and
- controlled refinement that preserves the approved narrative logic.

Architecture approval does **not** approve individual factual or causal claims, original frameworks not yet evidence-tested, chapter manuscripts, the final Change Capacity model, visual direction, the final manuscript or publication.

Material changes to chapter order, chapter purpose or the governing narrative arc require Alec Gardner's approval.
