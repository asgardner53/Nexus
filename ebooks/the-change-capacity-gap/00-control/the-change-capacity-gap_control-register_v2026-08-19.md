## Governing Proposition

> Organisations rarely experience change initiative by initiative. People experience the combined load. The Change Capacity Gap emerges when leaders govern transformations separately but the organisation must absorb them simultaneously. The leadership task is therefore to govern the total change load by sequencing, translating, removing, resourcing and stabilising change so that performance, judgement, trust and learning are not degraded.

**Status:** Approved by Alec Gardner, 19 August 2026.

## Originality Control

The project must not claim that “change capacity”, “change fatigue” or “change saturation” are newly invented concepts. The book's distinctive contribution is provisionally located in the governance mismatch between separately managed initiatives and cumulatively experienced organisational load, together with the practical leadership implications of governing total change demand.

Any proprietary model or framework must be evidence-tested before being described as original Nexus BMG intellectual property.
